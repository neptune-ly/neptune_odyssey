# Third-party licences — @neptune.fintech/icons

The **Odyssey icon family** in `src/icons.ts` is Neptune's own, hand-authored,
and licensed under the Neptune Odyssey Community License v1.0 (`LICENSE`).

The **three banks' sets** generated by `bank-sets/` are re-cut from two
permissively-licensed libraries. Both allow modification and redistribution with
the notice preserved; this file is that notice, and the Flutter apps register it
with `LicenseRegistry` so it is reachable from inside the shipped app.

| Library | Version | Licence | Holder |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) | 1.45.0 | **ISC** | © 2026 Lucide Icons and Contributors |
| ↳ its Feather-derived subset | — | **MIT** | © 2013–present Cole Bemis |
| [Tabler Icons](https://tabler.io/icons) | 3.46.0 | **MIT** | © 2020–2026 Paweł Kuna |

Neither licence requires attribution in the running UI; both require the notice
to travel with the redistribution.

## Brand marks

`src/brand-marks.ts` and the banks' `*_logo` / rail marks are **third-party
trademarks**, used to identify the service they name. They are not covered by
the licences above, are **never** restyled or redrawn to a bank profile, and a
mark belongs only to the banks that actually have that partner. See
`NOTICE-brand-marks.md`.
